Mitosis

The institute is a living organism

One bioluminescent cell — the RHRI mark itself — divides across a single scroll-driven canvas scene: 1 → 2 → 4 pillars → a clinic network → one coral heartbeat. ~100 words on the whole page.

  • Rebrand: abyssal black + bioluminescent teal + coral

05 / Specimen

Specimen

Lab brutalism — type as the graphic

A lab report blown up to poster scale: viewport-filling kinetic type that bulges around the cursor, stamp-slam pillar numerals, marquees, barcodes, and a rotated "RHRI VALIDATED" stamp.

  • Rebrand: bone paper + ink black + acid #D6FF3D
  • Type: variable-weight Archivo, all caps, letters touching
  • Signature: cursor-reactive headline, stamped reveals

06 / Deepfield

Deepfield

Signal v2 — fly through the network

You don't look at the network, you fly through it: scroll moves a camera through a 3D field of clinic nodes, from broken edges to a dense validated core, one sentence per station, instrument-grade HUD.

  • Evolved Signal DNA: deeper dark, glowing teal, coral beacon
